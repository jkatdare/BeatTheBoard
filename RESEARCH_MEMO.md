It wins, for now, and you should say so out loud rather than reaching for XGBoost.**\n\nThe arithmetic: ~165 NJT NYP departures/day × ~250 service days ≈ 41k labeled rows/year, over ~8 platform classes, with ~165 weekday train numbers. A GBM with `train_num` as a high-cardinality categorical on 41k rows will, with high probability, learn the per-train lookup table plus mild smoothing, and will do it less transparently and with worse calibration than doing it directly. Until you have (a) genuine occupancy features and (b) 6+ months across multiple disruption regimes, a **hierarchical empirical-Bayes smoothed lookup with recency weighting** is the correct model:\n\n```\nP(platform | x) ∝ Σ_levels λ_L · P̂_L(platform | backoff_key_L)\n```\nwith λ set by a Dirichlet/Jeffreys shrinkage on cell counts, then **masked by feasibility and renormalized**. Delay-conditioning enters as a *separate table over a coarse key* — `(line, hour_band, delay_bucket)` — because delay-conditioned cells are sparse and a joint model will overfit them instantly.\n\n**Where the GBM earns its keep:** once occupancy-simulation features exist (§3.5) and the turn map is populated, fit a multinomial logit or GBM over `[log P_lookup, occupancy features, delay features, turn features]` — i.e. **use the lookup posterior as a feature**, not as a competitor. Never train on physically-impossible classes — it wastes capacity and corrupts calibration. Keep the mask versioned per-day (out-of-service tracks change).\n\n**Calibrated three-state output.** Use **split conformal prediction with the APS/RAPS conformity score** ([Angelopoulos & Bates, arXiv:2107.07511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511); [Angelopoulos et al., ICLR 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14193)). APS builds the set by adding labels in descending calibrated-probability order until cumulative mass crosses the threshold — the resulting set is **arbitrary and non-contiguous by construction**, e.g. `{6,7,10,11,12}`, which is exactly the shape boxcarsignal already emits. You do not need a bespoke set-construction heuristic; you need a calibration split and a score.\n\nThen:\n- set size 1 → **point estimate**\n- 2 ≤ size ≤ 3 → **candidate set** (\"wait between platforms 4 and 6\")\n- size > 3 → **abstain**\n\nCritical detail: conformal gives *marginal* coverage.
